Hearth is a chat app for friend groups and communities: servers with text and voice channels, direct messages, group chats, and voice and video calls.

Messages and files are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they are sent, so the server stores them in a form it can't read. Calls are encrypted between the people in them.

The app connects to the free community server, kappachat.duckdns.org, by default. You can switch to any Hearth server you or your friends run: Settings → Apps & devices → Switch server. Hearth is open source (MIT), and the server runs on your own computer or a small VPS.

WHAT THE ANDROID APP ADDS
• Notifications about messages and calls, also after you swipe the app away. For that you need a UnifiedPush distributor app, such as ntfy (free). Without one, notifications only arrive while Hearth is open or in the background.
• Calls keep going with the screen locked, with an "In a call" notification that has a Hang up button.
• Invite links (hearth://invite/...) open in the app.
• Attachments and exports save to Downloads/Hearth.
• Links to other websites open in your browser. Only your Hearth server loads inside the app.
• If your server uses a self-signed certificate, the app shows its fingerprint, asks once, and then remembers that exact certificate.

IN HEARTH
• Servers with categories, roles and permissions, threads, replies, reactions, pins, polls and events.
• Direct messages, and group chats with up to 25 people.
• Voice and video calls, voice messages, and watch together: a video link, a live stream or a video file, in sync for everyone in the call.
• Profiles you can make your own: pictures, colors, fonts, effects and a full profile page.
• Disappearing messages, scheduled messages and reminders.
• Message search runs on your phone, so the server never sees the words you search for.
• Report and block people. Server owners get moderation tools.
• Two-factor sign-in and a recovery key.

NOT IN THE ANDROID APP YET
• Screen sharing.
• A full-screen incoming call screen. Calls ring as a notification.

PRIVACY
No ads and no tracking. The app has no analytics or ad libraries. Android's backup is off, so your sign-in and keys stay on your phone.
The server you use can see who is in which server, who messages whom and when, profiles, online status and IP addresses, but not what your messages and files say.
Some features contact other sites, which then see your IP address: the video players of the sites a watched video comes from, pictures from other sites that you choose to load, and the public STUN servers that help calls connect (the server's owner can change them).
