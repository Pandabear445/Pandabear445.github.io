Everything you'd expect

All the good parts of Discord. None of the creepy ones.

Hearth does what your group chat already needs, and every feature is free for everyone.

Encrypted by default

Messages, DMs and files are locked on your device before they're sent. Even whoever runs the server can't read them.

Voice, video and screen share

Drop into a voice channel, turn on your camera or share your screen with sound. Calls go straight between you and your friends.

Free, really

No ads. No upsells. Nothing held back.

Every feature on this page is free for everyone, with no "premium" tier to unlock it. Hearth is open source (MIT), so you can always run it yourself.

💜 How it pays for itself

  • People who want to can chip in, and get a supporter badge as a thank-you
  • A server costs a few dollars a month, and the numbers are shown to everyone
  • Creators can sell optional memberships to their own fans (a role, private channels), and Hearth keeps a small cut
  • No ads, no tracking and no selling your data, ever

🔒 What the server can't see

  • Your messages, DMs and files (they're encrypted on your device)
  • Your calls (they go directly between people in the call)
  • Your study decks (encrypted and synced like everything else)
Host your own

Your own Hearth in about ten minutes.

Run a server for your friends, your class or your community on a $5-a-month VPS or a spare computer. One server comfortably holds a busy community, and you can add call regions around the world later.

Get a small server

Any Linux VPS with Docker works (1 CPU and 1 GB is plenty to start), or your own PC.

Start Hearth

Download it and start it with Docker:

git clone https://github.com/Pandabear445/Pandabear445.github.io
cd Pandabear445.github.io/hearth
docker compose up -d

Invite your people

Point a domain at it for automatic HTTPS, create a server and share the invite link. Your friends get the apps from your own /download page.

Step-by-step guides for a VPS, a domain, call relays, backups and the apps are in the README.

Questions

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no paid features. Running a server costs a few dollars a month, and supporters who want to help cover that get a 💜 badge. Creators can also offer optional memberships to their own communities. Nobody has to pay for anything.

Can the people running the server read my messages?

No. Messages, DMs and files are encrypted in your app before they're sent, and only the people in that chat have the keys. The server stores scrambled data. Calls go directly between the people in them.

How is this different from Discord?

The everyday features are similar: servers, channels, voice, video, screen sharing, roles and GIFs. The differences: your chats are end-to-end encrypted, there are no ads or paid tiers, you can host it yourself, and profiles are a lot more fun.

Do I need to host it myself?

No. You can join the community server and start chatting right away. Hosting your own is for groups who want their own space and their own rules.

Is it open source?

Yes, it's MIT licensed. The code, the security notes and the setup guides are all on GitHub.
